Like many students, I was juggling university coursework at the Federal University of Technology Akure while searching for ways to earn income and build real skills.

I discovered Make.com and instantly fell in love with the power of automation - the ability to connect apps, eliminate manual work, and create systems that run 24/7.

What started as curiosity became a mission: help businesses work smarter, not harder.

Today, I'm building automations that help businesses save hours every week, allowing them to focus on serving their customers and growing their revenue.
